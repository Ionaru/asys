# The first version stays at weekly execution: no goals, projects or email

ASYS models Tasks, Activities, Occasions and Playbooks, and deliberately has no Goal or Project concept and no email features, although GRIP (which inspired ASYS) also plans at year level and treats email separately. Small undated projects are a parent Task with Subtasks and "blocked by" links, dated ones are Occasions, and a yearly review can be an Occasion made from a Playbook; a new top-level concept is only added once real use shows a need for it. Group was added on that basis, after a test with real scenarios showed that month-end chases per employee could not be expressed otherwise. ASYS does not schedule Tasks automatically either: the Picker suggests what fits the current Gap and Time blocks are placed by hand, with a manual "plan my day" as a possible later step. Sending messages (Slack or otherwise) and reading HR data (hours, leave, notice) are also out of scope: those steps stay manual Tasks with the draft text or a link in their Notes.
