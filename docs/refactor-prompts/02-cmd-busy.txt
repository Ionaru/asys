Move the per-screen in-flight tracking and the `pending || awaitingSync().has(subject)` check into `CommandAttempts`, as a `busy(subject)` method fed by `send`.

Why: `CommandAttempts.send` already folds key, send and settle, but each screen still hand-rolls the rest. Today that is eight in-flight signals and eight try/finally blocks: Now (`#pendingIds` in `#run`, read by `busy`), Inbox (`#pendingTaskIds` in `#runForCard`, `#pendingReviewIds` in `dismiss`, read by `cardBusy` and `reviewBusy`), TaskEditor (`#actionPending` in `#runAction`, `#removingIds` in `removeBlocker`, `#adding` in `addBlocker`, read by `actionsBusy`, `addBusy` and `removeBusy`), AreaEditor (`#sending` in `#runAction`, read by `canSubmit` beside `awaiting`) and Settings (`#pending` in `#run`, read by `busy`). Every new sending screen in slice 2 copies this, and each copy can drift in what it disables.

Current state: `apps/pwa/src/app/core/data/command-attempts.ts` has `keyFor`, `settle` and `send(command)`. `commandSubject(command)` in `core/data/command-subject.ts` names the subject `DataStore.awaitingSync()` uses (AddBlocker's subject is its Task id, RemoveBlocker's its link id). AreaEditor's `awaiting` keys on `areaId() ?? #pendingCreate()?.areaId`. `Settings.chooseZone` sends through `DataStore.chooseTimeZone`, not attempts. Capture's `busy` gates its form and reads no `awaitingSync`.

Change: give `CommandAttempts` a signal of in-flight subject counts that `send` raises and lowers in a try/finally on `commandSubject(command)`, and `busy(subject): boolean` returning in flight or `awaitingSync().has(subject)`. Add `track(subject, work)` for the zone choice. Screens drop their signals and try/finally and call `#attempts.busy(id)`, keeping their own extras (`leaving`, `#doneUndo.pending()`) on top. It stays in `core/data`.

Must stay identical: every control's disabled state and sync note in the screen specs, outcome handling, per-screen scope (`providers: [CommandAttempts]`), and DoneUndo, CaptureQueue and `DataStore` untouched.

Ask before you start: (1) An in-flight AddBlocker would now also disable Save, Done, Drop and Log progress, and a Save in flight would disable Add blocker: accept that, or keep TaskEditor's `#adding` signal? (2) Should `send` also own the post-await `#destroyRef.destroyed` check (eleven sites)? (3) Is Capture left as it is?
